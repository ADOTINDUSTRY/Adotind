The web edition stores the source pen paths, Unicode glyphs, advances and kerning groups.
During layout the editor interpolates shallow turns in the Signature, Capitol Script
and Handwritten pen paths into cubic curves through the original sampled points.
In Studio Sans, tiny cross-ticks centred on pen endpoints are removed when computing
the tube runs.
