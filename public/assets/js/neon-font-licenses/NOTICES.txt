Dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal.

https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Source releases:
https://gitlab.com/oskay/svg-fonts
https://github.com/golanlevin/p5-single-line-font-resources/tree/3b3082d3269548ae0032e51975bf0a3be9b67317/p5_single_line_svg_fonts/single_line_svg_fonts/EMS
https://github.com/isdat-type/Relief-SingleLine/tree/01dfc5779ec1e9e4b288d96c6c96c23bfccbaf9d

The web edition stores the source pen paths, Unicode glyphs, advances and kerning groups.
The designer fits smooth Bézier spans to the sampled pen paths of the EMS and NewStroke faces. Authored Bézier curves, pen lifts, straight stems and sharp corners are retained.
In Studio Sans, tiny cross-ticks centred on pen endpoints are removed when computing
the tube runs.
